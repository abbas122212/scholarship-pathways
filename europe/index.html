    Finish the internship

Erasmus Mundus · 16 programmes

Two-year joint master’s taught across two or three European universities. For a fresh MBBS graduate these are the best odds on the whole board. Every programme also has a full card under All routes (filter: Erasmus Mundus).

  • What it pays

    The standard Erasmus Mundus scholarship: €1,400 a month for up to 24 months, plus a waiver of the participation fee.

  • How you apply

    There is no central portal. Apply programme by programme, to up to three Erasmus Mundus programmes in one cycle.

  • When

    Windows run from October to January, each programme with its own dates. The table is sorted by closing date.

  • Watch out

    Not every joint master's carries the scholarship. Eu-HEM, for example, states the EMJM scholarship is not available.
