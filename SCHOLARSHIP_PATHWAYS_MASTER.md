## ITALY

**Italy — how it works (read this first):**
Italy is different from everywhere else on this page. There is no single big "Italy scholarship". Money comes from **your region**, based on **family income, not grades**. If your family's income is under the ceiling, the region pays you a yearly grant, exempts your tuition, and gives canteen meals and often housing. The catch: the paperwork (ISEE parificato) is slow, and you apply **after** you have a university place, in summer. Italy is therefore a **spring/summer 2027 play**, not a December race — but the income documents should be collected from now.

### 29. DSU Regional Scholarships (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) ⭐ Italy's main route

## GERMANY

**Germany — how it works (read this first):**
Public universities in Germany charge **no tuition fees** in most states (exception: Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students €1,500/semester). So in Germany the scholarship question is only about **living costs**. DAAD is the funder that matters. Without a scholarship, a student visa needs proof of funds of **€11,904/year** in a blocked account (2026 official figure, = €992/month — exactly the DAAD Master's rate).
