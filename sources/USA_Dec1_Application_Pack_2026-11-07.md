## 1. What each application actually requires (verified on official pages, 7 Nov 2026)

### Hopkins Bloomberg PhD Epidemiology — SOPHAS
- Fee **$155** (first programme; +$65 each additional). Fee waivers exist but must be requested *before* submitting and expire in 14 days; Epidemiology is not among the BSPH departments with their own waiver.
- **3 letters of recommendation required.** Trap: SOPHAS shows your file as "complete" with only 2 — Bloomberg still requires the third.
- Statement of Purpose and Objectives required. **No official word/page limit found** on JHU pages — check the prompt inside SOPHAS itself; do not trust the "2 pages" figure circulating on secondary sites.
- CV/resume required.
- **Credential evaluation is mandatory** for non-US records: only **WES ICAP ordered through SOPHAS**, or ECE sent directly to Bloomberg. **ECFMG certification is explicitly NOT accepted.** Degree in progress → evaluate completed coursework now, update later.
- English: TOEFL iBT **100** / IELTS **7.0** / Duolingo **130** / PTE **68**, scores under 2 years old. Waiver possible where a WES/ECE evaluation verifies English was the *only* language of instruction for your degree — but the waiver only applies once the evaluation is received. **Medium-of-instruction letters are explicitly NOT accepted.**
- GRE: not required, not reviewed.
- **Open gate (unchanged):** the programme FAQ states 1 year of full-time related work experience is required; whether your internship year counts is NOT stated anywhere official. The email to the department is still the only way to resolve it.
- No adviser needs to be secured before applying; faculty may be named in the SOP.

### Emory Rollins PhD Epidemiology — LaneyConnect (NOT SOPHAS)
- Fee **$75** (waivers exist).
- **3 letters of recommendation.**
- **Statement of purpose: 500 words**, with specified content — research experience/training that led you here, your research interests, how this programme addresses them, long-term goals after the doctorate.
- Also in the Laney application: a **Statement on Building Collaborative and Respectful Graduate Communities (≤500 words)** and an optional diversity statement.
- **You must name at least 2 Epidemiology graduate faculty** you want to work with. CV/resume required.
- **No credential evaluation needed to apply** — upload scanned registrar-issued transcripts (web printouts not accepted). Official transcripts only after admission. This removes the WES bottleneck for Emory entirely.
- English: TOEFL/IELTS required if your native language is not English; waived where your degree is from an institution where English is the language of instruction. A Laney-wide numeric minimum for Epidemiology is NOT FOUND — do not borrow another programme's number.
- GRE optional. ~200 applicants for 10–14 places; shortlisted candidates get a virtual visit day in February.

### Pittsburgh IBGP PhD — School of Medicine portal
- Fee **$50**; need-based waivers available.
- **Minimum 3 letters**, from faculty familiar with your research; the FAQ stresses your **research advisor's letter is very important**.
- Personal statement required — **no word limit or prompt on the official pages**; the FAQ warns the research description inside it is heavily weighed.
- CV: NOT FOUND as a listed requirement (include one anyway if the portal allows an upload).
- Transcripts uploaded in the application. **No credential-evaluation (WES) requirement stated.**
- English: TOEFL iBT **100** / IELTS **7.0** / Duolingo **120**; scores under 2 years old as of 1 Sep of the start term. Exemption only for a degree from a US or other approved English-instructed institution — treat Pittsburgh as needing a real score unless the exemption is confirmed.
- GRE not required. Minimum GPA 3.2 (incoming average 3.7). **All successful applicants have research experience, most ≥ 1 year in a lab** — the honest weak point of this file for a clinician; your manuscript portfolio is the counter-argument and must be front-loaded in the statement.
- Rolling review as files complete; interviews Jan–Mar (overseas by teleconference); offers by 15 April.

## 2. The letters you need

**Count: 3 strong letters + 1 backup referee** covers all three programmes (same referees can serve all three; each portal sends them its own upload link/request).

**Who (strongest combination for your profile):**
1. Your **research supervisor / senior co-author** — the Pittsburgh "research advisor" slot and the most important letter everywhere.
2. A **professor who taught or examined you and can rank you** against other students (committees look for comparative ranking, per Berkeley's guidance).
3. A **clinical/internship supervisor or senior collaborator** who can speak to work ethic, data work and reliability.
4. Backup: one more senior name, asked at the same time, in case someone goes silent — Berkeley's official advice is literally to line up one more referee than required.

**The briefing pack to hand each referee (this weekend):** your CV draft, your SOP draft (even rough), your transcript, a one-line description of each programme, the exact deadline, and 4–5 bullet points of specific things they personally saw you do (a dataset you cleaned, an analysis you ran, a deadline you saved). Generic letters are the known failure mode.

**Practical examples to copy the structure from (not the words):**
- Mirza Awais's recommendation-letter video (Facebook, 2:42) shows a **complete real letter on screen** and teaches how to personalise it instead of pasting a generic draft: https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=1116595870841004
- "Writing a Meaningful Letter of Recommendation" — a referee-facing guide (Berkeley Career Center guidelines; includes a **full doctoral-applicant template letter** and the exact briefing checklist): https://www.scribd.com/document/251902937/Writing-a-Meaningful-Letter-of-Recommendation — hand this PDF to your referees; it does the explaining for you.
- UC Berkeley's official letters page — how committees actually read letters, and to ask "can you write me a *strong* letter?": https://artshumanities.berkeley.edu/letters-recommendations

**Statements (the other "letters"):**
- Hopkins: research-weighted, no verified limit — model it on the admitted example below.
- Emory: hard **500 words**, must evidence fit with **2 named faculty**.
- Pittsburgh: research description carries the file; no limit found.
- Best model found anywhere: **Dr. Banda Khalifa reads his actual PhD statement that won funding at Johns Hopkins Bloomberg** and explains how a PhD statement differs from a Master's SOP (research first, chosen field, what you will advance): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jWanKecx3-Q
- Six annotated model MPH statements (commercial editing site — use the free annotations only): https://vincekotchian.com/personal-statements/mph-statement-of-purpose/

## 3. The timeline — Sat 7 Nov → Tue 1 Dec 2026

Deadlines are US Eastern; 1 Dec in Baltimore/Atlanta/Pittsburgh runs to ~2 Dec late morning Dhaka time. Do not plan on that buffer — plan to be submitted days early.

**This weekend (7–8 Nov) — the critical path starts**
- ☐ **Order WES ICAP through SOPHAS** (Hopkins) and get your university moving on sending mark sheets directly — WES's clock only starts when documents are *approved*, and the door-to-door reality is 4–6 weeks. This is the single longest pole; every day of delay comes off the other end.
- ☐ Open both accounts: **SOPHAS** and **LaneyConnect**.
- ☐ **Ask all 4 referees** (3 + backup) and send each the briefing pack. Standard advice is to ask 6–8 weeks out; you have 3½. Ask in person where possible.
- ☐ Decide the **English-test question**: if you hold no valid (<2 yrs) TOEFL/IELTS score, book the earliest sitting now — Pittsburgh almost certainly needs a score, Hopkins needs one unless the WES waiver lands in time, Emory's English-instruction waiver is the easiest of the three.
- ☐ Send the **Hopkins experience-gate email** (does the internship year satisfy the 1-year rule?) — a "no" answer is better learned this week than after paying $155.

**Week 2 (9–15 Nov) — drafts**
- ☐ CV built to academic format (Harvard guide + the CV videos below) — one master CV, trimmed per programme.
- ☐ SOP v1 for each programme: Hopkins (research narrative), Emory (500 words exactly, 2 faculty named — pick them from the Epi faculty list this week), Pittsburgh (research-description-led).
- ☐ Emory collaborative-communities statement drafted (≤500 words).
- ☐ Referees confirm they've accepted the portal requests; SOPHAS referee requests sent early so invitations are already in their inboxes.

**Week 3 (16–22 Nov) — files take shape**
- ☐ Letters landing: chase any referee who hasn't uploaded by 20 Nov; activate the backup referee by 22 Nov if one is silent.
- ☐ SOPs finalised; CV final; transcripts scanned to Emory's standard.
- ☐ **Submit Emory first** (no WES dependency — nothing to wait for once letters are in).
- ☐ Add Columbia inside SOPHAS (+$65) only if you decide the Sakib file is worth running this round.

**Week 4 (23–30 Nov) — submit everything**
- ☐ **Submit Hopkins on SOPHAS by ~25 Nov** — SOPHAS lets you submit before letters/transcripts arrive, but submit with all 3 letters already requested and WES moving; never submit on deadline day (portal congestion is a known failure mode).
- ☐ **Submit Pittsburgh by ~27 Nov** (rolling review rewards earlier complete files).
- ☐ Final sweep on 30 Nov: all three portals show the right status; third Hopkins letter confirmed *received*, not just requested.

**1 Dec** — deadlines pass with everything already in. Then the same pack, lightly edited, goes to **UNC by 15 Dec**.

**Honest timing note:** a fully *verified* SOPHAS file by 1 Dec is not achievable from a standing start on 7 Nov (WES approval + up to 10 business days of SOPHAS verification). What is achievable — and normal — is a complete, submitted file with WES verification following. Programmes run their own review on submitted files; the risk sits with Hopkins' WES-dependent English waiver, which is why a real test score, if you can get one booked, de-risks the whole round.
