# MRC North West Doctoral Programme in Medical Sciences — Application Pack
### Timeline, letters, projects, and the watch-in-full list
Built 7 Nov 2026, following the USA 1 December pack template (same five parts). Rule as always: official pages arbitrate; anything unverifiable is marked NOT FOUND.

**The target:** MRC North West Doctoral Programme in Medical Sciences (NW-DMS), University of Manchester + University of Liverpool — fully funded 4-year PhD, tuition + UKRI-rate stipend **£21,805/yr (2026/27 rate; 2027/28 rate may change)**, international applicants explicitly eligible, October 2027 start.
**Deadline: Friday 27 November 2026** — 20 days from today (Sat 7 Nov). Applications opened Fri 30 Oct 2026. Interviews w/c 8 Feb 2027; studentships awarded w/c 22 Feb 2027.
**Official programme page (verified 7 Nov 2026):** https://www.bmh.manchester.ac.uk/study/research/funding-fees/funded-programmes/mrc-nwdp/
**Enquiries:** fbmh.doctoralacademy.admissions@manchester.ac.uk

**Why this pack comes now, before the USA files are finished:** 27 Nov is *earlier* than the USA 1 Dec deadlines, and the MRC file has a different critical path — it is project-led, not portal-led. You apply to a named advertised project (up to two, each a separate application), and contacting the supervisor first is the expected step. The USA pack's weekend work (CV, referees) is reused here, so the extra cost is mainly project choice + supervisor emails + one tailored supporting statement per project.

## 1. What the application actually requires (verified on official pages, 7 Nov 2026)

- **Eligibility:** First or Upper Second-class UK honours degree (or international equivalent) in a relevant discipline, held or about to be obtained. Competition is very high; Manchester states many successful applicants may also have a master's qualification, relevant research experience, or experience demonstrating suitability for doctoral research. **No master's is required on the page.** Whether an MBBS (with internship still in progress) counts as the qualifying degree is NOT stated on the programme page — ask the Doctoral Academy admissions team in the same email as the supervisor contact, or separately; do not assume.
- **Studentships available to UK and international applicants** (Manchester programme page). Funding covers tuition fees plus a stipend at the UKRI rate for four years.
- **Projects:** projects for 2027/28 entry are live until Fri 27 Nov 2026, across biomedical and medical sciences at Manchester and Liverpool. **You can apply for up to two projects** as part of this programme.
- **How to apply (Manchester's step-by-step guide):** find a project, contact the supervisor, prepare CV / supporting statement / academic documents, apply through the University of Manchester portal. The programme page's step-by-step section did not render its detail in a plain fetch — the exact per-project document list must be confirmed inside the portal / step-by-step guide on the live page. Do not trust secondary-site checklists for this programme.
- **References:** programme-specific number NOT FOUND on the programme page. Manchester's standard for its PhD programmes is **two referees** (names + contact details; referees contacted after submission) — see e.g. Manchester PhD Chemistry / Audiology / Nursing application pages. Treat "2" as the working number and confirm it inside the MRC portal; the USA pack's 3 + 1 backup referee set covers it either way.
- **Supporting statement:** required (Manchester PhD standard: 1–2 pages — motivation, research area, why Manchester, relevant research/work experience, key findings and techniques from previous research). A programme-specific word limit for MRC NW-DMS is NOT FOUND — check the portal prompt; do not borrow another programme's limit.
- **Transcripts/certificates:** scanned registrar-issued transcripts and certificates for completed qualifications; interim transcript for a degree in progress. Official English translation if documents are not in English.
- **English:** a programme-specific minimum is NOT FOUND. Manchester Faculty of Biology, Medicine and Health guidance gives IELTS 6.5–7.0 as the general range — do not quote a number for this programme until the portal / admissions team confirms it. If you hold no valid (<2 yrs) IELTS/TOEFL score, ask admissions whether an English-medium MBBS qualifies for a waiver, in the same enquiry email.
- **Application fee:** NOT FOUND on the programme page or in search. Manchester postgraduate-research applications commonly carry no fee, but that is NOT verified for this programme — check inside the portal before assuming £0.
- **No WES / credential evaluation requirement stated** for this programme (unlike Hopkins SOPHAS). Do not order WES for Manchester.
- **No GRE requirement stated.**

**Honest fit note:** this is a biomedical-science DTP, and most advertised projects are lab-based. Your fit is decided by project choice, not by the programme title. Population, clinical-data, modelling and prescribing projects (shortlist below) are where an MBBS + your manuscript portfolio is a strength; a wet-lab molecular project would be a weak file. Pick the project first, then write the statement to it.

## 2. Project shortlist — closest to your profile (from the live 2027/28 project list via FindAPhD, 7 Nov 2026)

These titles were found on the live project listings; **confirm each one on Manchester's official projects page** (linked from the programme page above) before emailing — project descriptions, supervisor emails and any MBBS-relevant prerequisites live there, and the plain-fetch version of the page did not expose the full list.

1. **Rebuilding trust in vaccination: a Large Epidemic Model to predict immunity gaps and power trustworthy public health communication** — University of Liverpool. Supervisors: Dr S Chen, Prof A J Jorgensen. *Best fit in the whole list:* epidemic modelling + public-health communication; measles returning is the framing. Your dengue / cold-mortality trend work and GBD experience map directly onto it.
2. **Closing the loop: an AI-guided approach to prioritise competence experimentation of emerging arbovirus risk** — University of Liverpool. *Strong fit:* arbovirus / emerging-infection risk; pairs with your dengue work.
3. **Genomic and mathematical approaches for the precise targeting of public health interventions to control viral emergence** (Precision Medicine DLA) — *strong fit:* public-health intervention targeting, modelling.
4. **Antibiotic use in hidradenitis suppurativa: prescribing patterns, treatment trajectories and antimicrobial treatment failure** — University of Manchester. *Good fit:* prescribing / AMR / treatment trajectories — clinical, data-led, no wet lab implied by the title.
5. **Developing and testing personalised approaches to understand and reduce indoor environmental exposures during pregnancy** — University of Manchester. *Good fit:* exposure epidemiology in pregnancy; clinical-population angle.
6. **A digital portal incorporating patient-reported outcome measures to improve the equity and effectiveness of outpatient epilepsy care** — *good fit:* health-services / equity, patient-reported outcomes.
7. **Optimising data completeness in clinical prediction models incorporating high-frequency patient-generated health data** — University of Manchester. *Good fit:* clinical prediction modelling, data completeness — your NHANES / GBD data work is the argument.
8. **Reliable Multimodal Agentic AI for Cardiovascular Disease Diagnosis and Risk Prediction** — University of Manchester. *Adjacent fit:* cardiovascular risk prediction; your cardiometabolic manuscript is the link, but the AI-methods weight is high.

Do not apply to more than two — and do not pick two lab-heavy projects as your pair. Suggested pairing: **(1) vaccination epidemic model + (2) arbovirus risk** (both Liverpool, both public-health modelling), or **(1) + (4) antibiotic prescribing** if you want a Manchester + Liverpool spread. A second application needs its own tailored supporting statement.

**Supervisor email — send this weekend (8–9 Nov), template:**
Subject: Prospective MRC NW-DMS applicant — [exact project title]
Body (150–200 words, attach academic CV): who you are (Bangladeshi MBBS, internship, Chattogram); one line naming a specific paper/project of theirs and why it matters; two lines on you (degree, one relevant skill, one result with numbers — e.g. a submitted manuscript / dataset analysed); the connection to their project and one question showing you read it; the ask — are they open to a brief call, and is an MBBS applicant without a prior master's a realistic fit for this project? Close with the 27 Nov deadline so they know the timing. One polite reminder after a week with no reply, then move on — supervisors decide in seconds, and a generic mass email is spotted and ignored (see Codanics guide, §4 below).

## 3. The letters you need

**Count: the USA pack's 3 strong + 1 backup referees cover this file too** (Manchester standard is 2). Same referees, same briefing pack — ask once, use twice. If you have not yet asked them for the USA files, this weekend's ask covers both deadlines; tell each referee both sets of dates (MRC 27 Nov; Hopkins/Emory/Pittsburgh 1 Dec) so no one is surprised by a second portal link a week later.

**Who (same as the USA pack):** (1) research supervisor / senior co-author — the most important letter everywhere; (2) a professor who taught/examined you and can rank you against other students; (3) a clinical/internship supervisor or senior collaborator; (4) backup senior name, asked at the same time.

**Briefing pack to hand each referee:** CV draft, SOP/supporting-statement draft (even rough), transcript, a one-line description of each programme/project, the exact deadline, and 4–5 bullet points of specific things they personally saw you do. Practical examples (reused from the USA pack — they are programme-neutral): Mirza Awais's real-letter video, https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=1116595870841004 ; Berkeley referee guide with full doctoral template letter, https://www.scribd.com/document/251902937/Writing-a-Meaningful-Letter-of-Recommendation ; UC Berkeley letters page, https://artshumanities.berkeley.edu/letters-recommendations

## 4. The timeline — Sat 7 Nov → Fri 27 Nov 2026

**This weekend (7–8 Nov) — shared with the USA critical path**
- ☐ CV to academic format (one master CV — reuse for USA + MRC; see watch list).
- ☐ Ask all 4 referees, naming both the MRC (27 Nov) and USA (1 Dec) deadlines.
- ☐ Browse Manchester's official MRC projects page; shortlist 2 projects from §2 above and read their full descriptions.
- ☐ Decide the English-test question (see §1) — one enquiry email to fbmh.doctoralacademy.admissions@manchester.ac.uk can settle MBBS eligibility + English waiver + fee in a single ask.

**Week 2 (9–15 Nov) — project + supervisor**
- ☐ Supervisor emails sent by Mon 9–Tue 10 Nov at the latest (supervisors need time to reply before you write a statement to their project).
- ☐ Supporting statement v1 per project (1–2 pages, Manchester standard): why this project, what your research experience actually was (problem, findings, techniques), why Manchester/Liverpool, what you would do next.
- ☐ USA drafts continue in parallel (Emory 500-word SOP is the tightest item that week).

**Week 3 (16–22 Nov) — files take shape**
- ☐ Chase any supervisor who has not replied by ~17 Nov; do not wait past 19 Nov to commit to your two projects.
- ☐ Statements finalised per project; transcripts scanned; referees confirm they have accepted the portal requests.
- ☐ Submit Emory (USA pack) in this window — nothing to wait for there.

**Week 4 (23–27 Nov) — submit**
- ☐ **Submit MRC by Wed 25 Nov** — do not plan on deadline day (portal congestion is a known failure mode; and 27 Nov is also the week the USA files go in).
- ☐ Submit Hopkins by ~25 Nov, Pittsburgh by ~27 Nov (USA pack).
- ☐ Final sweep 26 Nov: both MRC applications show the right project, statement, and referee details; second project application filed separately if you are running two.

**After 27 Nov:** interviews w/c 8 Feb 2027; awards w/c 22 Feb 2027; start Oct 2027. Then the same pack, lightly edited, goes to Commonwealth Shared (closes 8 Dec), Maastricht (file complete before 10 Dec), UNC (15 Dec).
