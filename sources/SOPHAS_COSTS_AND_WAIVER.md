# SOPHAS Fee Waiver, MPH Costs, and Applying with a Degree in Progress

Researched 3 November 2026. Evidence is from official pages only (SOPHAS/Liaison help centre, university financial-aid, registrar, ISSS/OIS and programme pages). Aggregators were used only as search leads, never as evidence. Anything not found on an official page is marked NOT FOUND.

## 1. SOPHAS fee waiver — wording and availability mechanics

Source: SOPHAS Fees and Fee Waivers, Liaison Applicant Help Center
https://help.liaisonedu.com/SOPHAS_Applicant_Help_Center/Starting_Your_SOPHAS_Application/Getting_Started_with_Your_SOPHAS_Application/03_Application_Fees
(page updated 2026-08-06; fees shown: "$155 for the first program you apply to and $65 for each additional program")

### What the page says about the fund

- "Beginning at the start of the cycle, a limited number of fee waivers are provided to qualified applicants on a first-come, first-served basis."
- "You may request a fee waiver as soon as you create an application, but keep in mind that **fee waiver funds can be quickly depleted**. **Contact customer service at 617-612-2090 before you submit a fee waiver request to determine if waivers are still available.**"
- The page gives **no** statement of when funds will run out and **no** public counter of remaining waivers. The official mechanism for checking availability is to call SOPHAS customer service (617-612-2090) before requesting — i.e., remaining availability is not published on the page.
- "You must have an account to apply for a fee waiver. Fee assistance applications are not accepted by mail or email." The request itself is made inside the application (Fee Waiver Form section). The help page does not display live availability; it directs applicants to customer service for that.

### Confirmed mechanics

- **Covers the first designation only:** "Each fee waiver covers only the initial application fee, so if you wish to apply to additional programs, you are responsible for the remaining balance." The international waiver form states: "If awarded, this fee waiver covers only the initial application fee ($155)."
- **$80 WES coupon:** "You can also request a coupon for $80 off your WES evaluation in the form. Note that the $80 credit is only valid for your initial WES evaluation order. Once your order is placed, no refunds are possible." The form item reads: "I am requesting an $80 credit for a WES course-by-course evaluation."
- **14-day expiry:** "If you are approved for a fee waiver, you are required to submit your application within 14 days (including holidays and weekends). Extensions will not be granted; failure to submit your application on time will void your fee waiver and you will not be allowed to reapply for a waiver." Note: "Outstanding transcripts, recommendations, and/or test scores do not have to be received within this timeframe." Processing takes "up to 10 business days."
- **Must request before submitting:** "you must request a fee waiver **before** you submit your application. Requests received after an application has been submitted will not be honored." One waiver type per cycle.

### Bangladesh eligibility — confirmed on the page itself

- Eligibility rule: "To qualify for an international-based fee waiver, you must be a citizen of **and** currently reside in one of the countries on the United Nations Least Developed Countries List below."
- The list printed on the SOPHAS help page includes **Bangladesh** (second entry, after Afghanistan and Angola — listed alphabetically as Afghanistan, Angola, Bangladesh, Benin, …).
- The 2026 international waiver form states the same terms: "This fee waiver is only available to international applicants who are citizens of and currently reside in one of the countries on the United Nations Least Developed Countries List… Fee waivers are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis. There are a limited number of fee waivers available."
  Form: https://help.liaisonedu.com/@api/deki/files/1529/SOPHAS_Service_Waiver_Request_International_Applicants_2026.pdf?revision=9

**Bottom line on availability:** the waiver is genuinely first-come-first-served from a limited pool and SOPHAS itself warns funds "can be quickly depleted" — but there is no official statement that the Fall 2027-cycle pool is exhausted, and no public way to see remaining funds. The official route to find out is: create the account, and/or call 617-612-2090 before submitting the request. Because approval starts a non-extendable 14-day clock to submit the application, the request should only be made when the application is essentially ready to submit.

## 3. Applying while the bachelor's degree (MBBS) is still in progress

**Answer: Yes — you apply during the final phase of the degree; what the universities require is degree completion by enrolment/matriculation, not by the application date. None of the four MPH programmes requires work experience or internship completion for admission.**

### SOPHAS mechanics (Liaison help centre)

- In-progress/planned coursework is a normal part of the application: the Transcript Entry instructions require reporting repeated coursework "including those that are currently in-progress or planned," and coursework is entered term by term with in-progress/planned status.
  https://help.liaisonedu.com/SOPHAS_Applicant_Help_Center/Starting_Your_SOPHAS_Application/Getting_Started_with_Your_SOPHAS_Application/00_SOPHAS_Quick_Start_Guide
- "You can submit your application before your transcripts and recommendations are received." (Submitting and Completing Your SOPHAS Application) — submission is not blocked on final documents being in.
  https://help.liaisonedu.com/SOPHAS_Applicant_Help_Center/Submitting_and_Monitoring_Your_SOPHAS_Application/Before_and_After_You_Submit_Your_SOPHAS_Application/01_Submitting_and_Completing_Your_SOPHAS_Application
- After submission, the **Academic Update** window allows updating planned/in-progress coursework once completed: "Academic Update allows you to update planned or in-progress US and English-Canadian coursework that you completed since your application was originally verified… During the Academic Update window, you can also enter new planned/in-progress US and English-Canadian coursework for the upcoming term(s)." For the 2026–27 cycle the AU window is 21 June – 30 July 2027, with updated transcripts due by 5 August 2027. Caveat: Academic Update is written for US/English-Canadian coursework; a Bangladeshi MBBS is handled through the foreign-evaluation (WES) route, so the practical update path for foreign credentials is the updated evaluation/transcript per programme instructions — programme-specific confirmation is advisable.
  https://help.liaisonedu.com/SOPHAS_Applicant_Help_Center/Submitting_and_Monitoring_Your_SOPHAS_Application/Before_and_After_You_Submit_Your_SOPHAS_Application/03_Updating_Your_SOPHAS_Application%2F%2FAcademic_Update

### University wording — degree by enrolment, not by application

- **Emory Rollins (Admissions FAQ):** "Students who are near completion of a baccalaureate (or higher) degree may apply during their final year and submit incomplete transcripts. However, an offer of admission is contingent upon receipt of an applicant's final transcript, which clearly documents completion of the degree."
  https://sph.emory.edu/admissions/faq
- **Georgia State (Graduate Catalog, MPH Application Requirements):** "Applicants must have a bachelor's degree or its equivalent **at the time of enrollment** from a regionally accredited institution."
  https://catalogs.gsu.edu/preview_entity.php?catoid=39&ent_oid=2816&returnto=5118
- **Drexel Dornsife:** "Prior to enrolling in the program, applicants must have completed a bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited college or university." (Dornsife MPH programme pages, drexel.edu)

### Work experience / internship completion — not an admission requirement at any of the four

- **Drexel (explicit):** "The MPH programs do not require work experience. Your classmates will have a mix of varied experiences." Required: bachelor's degree (or equivalent international institution), GPA 3.0 preferred, statistics recommended.
  https://drexel.edu/dornsife/academics/degrees/mph-degree/
- **Emory Rollins:** published application materials are personal statement, resume, two letters of recommendation, college transcripts; "The GRE is not required for admission and will not be considered." No work-experience or internship-completion requirement appears in the Rollins FAQ or departmental MPH requirements (epidemiology asks for one college-level maths and one science course, "recommended, but not required"; preferred GPA 3.0).
  https://sph.emory.edu/admissions/faq ; https://sph.emory.edu/degrees-programs/mph/epidemiology
- **Georgia State:** required materials are SOPHAS application, resume/CV, transcripts, three references, statement of purpose, TOEFL/IELTS for international applicants; "The GRE is not a requirement for admission to the MPH program." No work-experience requirement is listed.
  https://publichealth.gsu.edu/program/epidemiology-mph/ (and other concentration pages)
- **UIC (Academic Catalog, MPH – Community Health Sciences):** Admission requirements are "Prior Degree: Bachelor's degree in any field," GPA typically ≥3.00/4.00, "GRE not required," three letters, resume/CV, personal statement, English proficiency. No work-experience requirement for the standard (Comprehensive) MPH. (UIC's separate Professional Enhancement Program track is designed for experienced professionals — it is an optional track, not the standard admission route.)
  https://catalog.uic.edu/gcat/colleges-schools/public-health/mph/chs/

**Practical implication for an MBBS intern:** an applicant can apply through SOPHAS while the MBBS/internship is still being completed, listing in-progress study; admission offers are contingent on the final transcript documenting degree completion before enrolment. Internship completion is therefore a *matriculation* timeline question (degree must be finished by the time the programme starts), **not** an application-eligibility or work-experience requirement, at all four schools checked.
