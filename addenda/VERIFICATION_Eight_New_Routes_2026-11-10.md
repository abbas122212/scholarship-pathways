## N1. Penn — PhD in Epidemiology & Biostatistics — PARTIAL
- CONFIRMED: applications through 1 Dec 2026 for Fall 2027; GRE not required; stipend **$49,000** (Penn Biomedical Graduate Studies, effective Fall 2026). https://www.med.upenn.edu/ggeb/epiadmissions.html
- **Not "fully funded for the whole PhD" as written.** For Epidemiology, Penn describes a fellowship for an initial 16 months, after which the student must secure a dissertation project with confirmed funding. This comes from a search summary of Penn's pages, so read the Epidemiology page for the exact wording.
- **Risk for an MBBS holder:** Penn's BGS admissions FAQ says applicants who already hold a professional degree must secure their own funding and BGS cannot offer financial support. That FAQ is for BGS programmes and may not govern GGEB Epidemiology, but it must be asked about in writing. https://www.med.upenn.edu/bgs/admissions-faqs.html
- The note that international applicants have no citizenship restriction: no restriction was found, but nothing affirmative either.

## N2. Manchester — International Partnership PhD Studentships — PARTIAL
- CONFIRMED: applications open 9 Nov 2026, close **11 Dec 2026**; start October 2027; interviews week of 22 Feb 2027; awards week of 8 Mar 2027; up to two projects, each a separate application. https://www.bmh.manchester.ac.uk/study/research/funding-fees/funded-programmes/international-partnership-phd/

## N3. UBC — Four Year Doctoral Fellowship — PARTIAL
- CONFIRMED: from September 2026 the 4YF pays **$24,000 a year plus tuition for up to four years**; domestic and international students can hold it. https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/four-year-doctoral-fellowship-4yf
- CORRECTION: UBC says **all PhD applicants offered admission are automatically considered**, and each programme gets a set number of places. This is not a separate nomination you chase.
- NOT VERIFIED: the "International Doctoral Fellowship $30,000/year" in the file. It was not found on UBC's 4YF page, so do not cite it.

## N4. Oxford — Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships — PARTIAL
- CONFIRMED: Bangladesh is on Oxford's eligible-countries list for 2027–28. Applicants must be offered a place and be ordinarily resident in an eligible country. https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/fees-funding-and-scholarship-search/weidenfeld-hoffmann-scholarships-and-leadership-programme
- NEW CATCHES from Oxford's page: applicants held over to the **March** deadline or on a waiting list are **not** eligible; candidates with deferred offers are **not** eligible.
- NOT VERIFIED: that MSc Global Health Science and Epidemiology, MSc International Health and Tropical Medicine, MSc Health Service Improvement and Evaluation and the MPP are on the eligible-course list. The course list was not read.
- NOT VERIFIED: "living-costs grant of at least £21,805". Oxford pages read gave "at least £20,780" and "at least £18,622" (college-specific). £21,805 is the UKRI 2026/27 stipend, so the figure may have been copied from there.
