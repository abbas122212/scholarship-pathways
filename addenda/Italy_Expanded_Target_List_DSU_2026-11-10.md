## The DSU scholarship — how it actually works

- **What it is:** a *regional*, **needs-based** (not merit-based) right-to-study grant. Each region runs its own call through its own body (table below). A winning ("idoneo") student typically gets: full tuition exemption, a cash grant paid in instalments, free/cheap canteen meals, and sometimes a residence place.
- **2026/27 national frame (MUR, pattern for 2027/28):** ISEE ceiling €28,339.88 and ISPE ceiling €61,608.48 nationally; the national *fuori sede* (living away from home) minimum grant was €7,171.11/year, with lowest-band total packages around €8,246–€8,248/year including meal value. **Individual calls set their own thresholds** — e.g. Padua's verified call thresholds are ISEE < €26,306.25 and ISPE < €43,125.94. Treat every figure as region- and cycle-specific.
- **International applicants do not use a normal ISEE.** You need an **ISEE parificato**: your family's income/asset documents from Bangladesh, legalised and translated, converted by a CAF office in Italy (for Padua, only the university/ESU-affiliated CAF, free of charge). This is the slowest step — start collecting the Bangladeshi documents months ahead.
- **Timing:** there is **no 2027/28 DSU call published anywhere yet** (checked 10 Nov 2026). Calls follow enrolment — 2026/27 calls ran roughly June–September 2026 by region (Padua's deadline was 30 September 2026). Expect a similar mid/late-2027 window; the university place comes first.
- **Merit condition to keep the money:** instalments after the first depend on credits earned. Padua's verified rule: the second instalment is paid only after **20 ECTS by 10 August**, and the grant is revoked if 20 ECTS is not reached by 30 November.
- **Cannot be combined** with MAECI or Invest Your Talent in Italy for the same period, and Padua's International Excellence Scholarship cannot be held with DSU either.
- **Honest flag:** the widely quoted "€8,246/year" fuori-sede maximum could not be found in Padua's official call text during this check (it appears in aggregators and a Facebook screenshot of a table). It is recorded as NOT CHECKED for Padua specifically; the national-frame figure above comes from the MUR 2026/27 ceilings recorded earlier.

## Step-by-step process — from today to the DSU grant

1. **Pick the programme, not just the university.** Choose 3–6 programmes from the list below where the MBBS-eligibility line names Medicine or a generic bachelor's you clearly satisfy. If it does not name Medicine, email the programme office *before* paying the fee.
2. **Apply on the university's own portal** inside its 2027/28 window (table above; portal link per university below). Upload the documents listed for that university, pay the application fee, submit. Keep the submission receipt.
3. **Get your qualification recognised.** Order a **CIMEA** Statement of Comparability / Verification online (the vetted Bengali videos below put the cost at €300–€400, matching the official range), *or* a Declaration of Value (DoV) from the Italian Embassy — CIMEA is the route most universities on this list accept and is done from Bangladesh. Some universities ask for this at application, others at enrolment — the per-university documents list says which.
4. **Admission letter.** If admitted, accept the place and pay any enrolment deposit/first instalment the university requires.
5. **Universitaly pre-enrolment.** Register on universitaly.it, submit the pre-enrolment application for your one chosen programme, and wait for the university to validate it. No visa step works without a validated pre-enrolment.
6. **Student visa (type D) from Bangladesh.** Apply through the Embassy of Italy in Dhaka's visa channel with the validated Universitaly summary, admission letter, CIMEA/DoV, financial proof, accommodation proof and insurance. *The exact Dhaka checklist and appointment route change over time and were NOT re-verified line-by-line in this pass — check the embassy/VFS checklist at the time you apply; the vetted visa videos below show the current shape of the process.*
7. **Arrive and enrol.** Complete enrolment, pay the first tuition instalment (DSU winners are exempt/refunded per the call), get your **codice fiscale**, and apply for the **permesso di soggiorno** (residence permit) within 8 days of arrival.
8. **ISEE parificato.** Take your prepared Bangladeshi family income/asset documents (legalised + translated) to a CAF office — or the university/DSU-affiliated CAF where the call requires it — and get the ISEE parificato issued.
9. **Apply for DSU** on your regional body's portal inside its call window (expected mid/late 2027), with the ISEE parificato, enrolment details and bank details. Rankings are published; winners are paid in instalments tied to the ECTS merit rule.
10. **Keep the grant.** Hit the credit thresholds in your call (Padua pattern: 20 ECTS by 10 Aug / 30 Nov) and re-apply each year.

### Documents — master checklist

**For the university application (all 20, per their official pages — exact lists per university below):**
- Passport (valid ID)
- MBBS degree certificate / diploma — or provisional/enrolment certificate if the degree is not yet in hand where the portal allows it
- Full transcript of records (English or Italian translation where required)
- CV / résumé in English
- Motivation letter / statement of purpose (most portals)
- English certificate at the level the programme states (mostly B2; Padua Food, Nutrition and Health requires C1 or an English-taught degree)
- Recommendation letter(s) where the programme requires them
- Any programme-specific document (portfolio, entrance-test registration — e.g. CISIA CEnT-S for some Padua programmes)

**For CIMEA / recognition:** degree certificate + transcript (legalised/apostilled copies as CIMEA instructs), passport, and the CIMEA application fee.

**For Universitaly + visa:** validated pre-enrolment summary, admission letter, CIMEA/DoV, passport, photos, proof of financial means, proof of accommodation in Italy, health insurance. *(Dhaka-specific checklist: verify at application time — see step 6.)*

**For ISEE parificato / DSU — prepare these in Bangladesh well ahead:**
- Family composition certificate
- Family income documents for the reference year (employment/income certificates for each earning member)
- Family asset/property documents and bank statements
- All of the above legalised and translated into Italian, then converted by a CAF in Italy
- After arrival: codice fiscale, enrolment certificate, residence-permit receipt, Italian bank account/IBAN

## Blind spots and limits of this check

- **Facebook group interiors could not be browsed** by the search tools used — only public posts, pages, videos and Reels that search surfaces were checked (leads found for 20/20 universities). Bengali Facebook group interiors remain unchecked and are best browsed in your own signed-in app.
- 21 of 327 leads are marked NOT CHECKED, each with its reason in the per-university lists (mostly an official page that rate-limited or would not render). No figure from those leads was used anywhere in the deadline table.
- 14 universities have not published 2027/28 calls yet; their windows/fees shown are the 2026/27 cycle, labelled as such. A re-check is needed when the calls drop (most expected Dec 2026–Feb 2027), and again for every DSU call in mid/late 2027.
- Video published dates were NOT FOUND for many uploads (the platforms did not expose them to the checker); verdicts rest on the process shown, not the upload date.
