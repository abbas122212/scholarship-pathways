### N2. University of Manchester — International Partnership PhD Studentships
- **Status:** Opened **9 November 2026**, closes **11 December 2026**; start October 2027
- **Funding:** 4 years — UKRI-rate stipend + tuition at home **or international** rate + £5,000/year research training support grant + £1,000 travel
- **Eligibility:** Project-tied (studentships attach to specific projects with partner universities). **No 2027/28 epidemiology/public-health project was visible when checked — the project list, published from 9 Nov, must be watched**
- **Source:** University of Manchester official studentship pages — verified 9 Nov 2026
- **Note:** Distinct from the MRC North West Doctoral Programme (deadline 27 Nov 2026) — a separate Manchester route with a later deadline.

### N3. University of British Columbia — Four Year Doctoral Fellowship (4YF), Canada
- **Status:** Running scheme; nomination-based — there is no central applicant deadline, the PhD programme's own deadline governs
- **Funding:** From September 2026: **$24,000/year stipend + full tuition for 4 years**; international students eligible. Same official page: International Doctoral Fellowship — $30,000/year + tuition × 4 years
- **Eligibility:** PhD only; awarded by nomination after programme admission. UBC's School of Population and Public Health is the natural home programme
- **Source:** UBC Graduate Studies official fellowship pages — verified 9 Nov 2026

### N4. University of Oxford — Weidenfeld–Hoffmann Scholarships
- **Status:** Considered automatically with the Oxford graduate application — **deadline = your course deadline (January 2027 for the health MScs below)**
- **Funding:** **100% of course fees + a living-costs grant of at least £21,805**
- **Eligibility:** Bangladesh is on Oxford's official eligible-countries list. Eligible courses explicitly include **MSc Global Health Science and Epidemiology**, **MSc International Health and Tropical Medicine**, **MSc Health Service Improvement and Evaluation**, and the MPP
- **Source:** University of Oxford official scholarship pages — verified 9 Nov 2026

### N5. University of Cambridge — Cambridge Trust International Scholarship (PhD)
- **Status:** No separate application — you are considered through the Cambridge course application; the **course funding deadline governs**
- **Funding:** International-rate tuition + college fee + annual maintenance + visa costs and Immigration Health Surcharge
- **Eligibility:** Any subject **except premium-rate courses**; any nationality except UK/Ireland
- **Source:** Cambridge Trust / University of Cambridge official pages — verified 9 Nov 2026
- **Note:** The Trust's Master's-level award covers fees only, with no maintenance — it **fails** the fully-funded filter and is not a route.

### N8. Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarships (QECS) — ACU
- **Status:** **Cycle 1 opens November/December 2026**; the 2027 host-university list is not yet published
- **Funding:** Fully funded 2-year Master's in a **low- or middle-income Commonwealth country** (not UK/Canada/Australia): tuition + living allowance + return flights + arrival allowance + research grant
- **Eligibility:** A 2:1-equivalent first degree is required
- **Source:** Association of Commonwealth Universities official QECS pages — verified 9 Nov 2026

### N9. ⭐ WATCH — UCAS Master's → CAS PhD stepping stone, China
- **Status: WATCH (added at Abbas's instruction, 10 Nov 2026).** The 2027 UCAS degree call was **NOT FOUND as of 10 Nov 2026**
- **Why a stepping stone, not a direct route:** Direct UCAS PhD entry **FAILED** for an MBBS-only applicant — UCAS's own 2026 call requires PhD applicants to submit **both** a Master's and a Bachelor's certificate. A funded UCAS **Master's** (a Bachelor's/MBBS qualifies) followed by a CAS PhD is the real path
- **Funding (UCAS Scholarship, full):** tuition waived (PhD tuition value RMB 40,000/year) + stipend **RMB 2,500/month** plus supervisor/institute top-up to **at least RMB 3,000/month (Master's) / RMB 3,500/month (PhD)** + basic accommodation + RMB 800/year medical insurance, up to 36 months. CAS-ANSO also funds Master's students (100–200/year) and PhDs
- **2026 pattern (last cycle, pattern only):** programme applications 15 Oct 2025 – 15 Apr 2026; scholarship deadline 31 Jan 2026
- **Watch for:** publication of the 2027 UCAS call; suitable English-taught health/biomedical Master's programmes at UCAS institutes
- **Source:** UCAS official 2026 call and scholarship pages — verified 10 Nov 2026 (via the GradStories doomscroll verification)
