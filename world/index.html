The plan in one screen

Three things shape every route

Bangladeshi MBBS (2019–20 session), results out September 2026, internship from Oct–Nov 2026 in Chattogram, no work experience, USD 30k budget. Long-term goal: practise medicine in the US. Fields: public health, epidemiology, global health, clinical research, aerospace medicine.

  1. 1

    Finish the internship

    Without it there is no full BMDC registration, so no FCPS, full GMC, Saudi, Qatar or UAE licence, and no AHPRA registration. Realistic time to leave: December 2027 to April 2028.

  2. 2

    Best bridge to the US

    Take Step 1 during the internship, then a paid US research job, then Step 2 CK, aiming for the 2029 Match. That keeps the graduation recent (about 3 years) and earns US letters of recommendation.

  3. 3

    Apply now, start later

    Erasmus Mundus, Gates Cambridge, KAUST, MEXT, GKS, Stipendium Hungaricum, Türkiye and CSC need no work experience. Most 2027 starts collide with the internship, so ask each programme about late arrival or aim for 2028.

Left out on purpose

Why there is no China here

Mainland China is excluded unless a route is a free or paid trip, or highly beneficial and easy to get. Both mainland routes are still on the full board.

  • Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

    Free. A full degree in mainland China. Not a short or paid trip, and neutral for the US goal, so it is left off.

  • Yenching Academy, Peking University

    Free: tuition, housing, monthly stipend, airfare. Fully paid including airfare, but it is a China Studies master's, not health, and a hard selection. Left off.

  • Kept: Hong Kong PhD Fellowship (HKPFS)

    Hong Kong runs its own system and HKPFS pays a salary (≈USD 44k a year plus travel), so it stays on this site as the exception. It is a long shot (2/10).

Unanswered

Open questions that decide several routes

Each one is worth an email. The answer can open or close several routes at once.

  1. When is the MBBS conferred?

    Before or only after the internship? It decides SpaceMed, Commonwealth Shared, Maastricht and others that need the degree finished by a deadline.

    Bachelor's route (GRE needed) or bachelor's + master's route (no GRE)? Does his average convert to at least 7.5/10? Ask masteradmission-fhml@maastrichtuniversity.nl.

  3. Swedish Institute's 3,000 hours

    Paid or freelance work separate from the internship, before Feb 2027. The SI FAQ says internships don't count.

  4. Hopkins work-experience line

    The one-year line appears on the ScM page, not the PhD page. Ask BSPH.EpiAcademic@jh.edu whether the internship counts.

    No university says an MBBS alone qualifies. It's case by case (publications, research), with an MPhil as fallback.

    Starting in early November lands mid-December — too late for a verified 1 Dec file. Ask Hopkins and Emory if WES can follow.
